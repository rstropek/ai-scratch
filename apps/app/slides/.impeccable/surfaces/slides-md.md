## Scope and mode

The Slidev deck in `slides/` that Rainer records into the Digitalferien 2026 video tutorial. Mode: Read (kids understand one idea per slide while Rainer narrates). 16:9, watched on school laptops inside a video.

## Audience, job, constraints

Kids 10–16, Scratch beginners. Each slide anchors one idea of the narration: welcome, how the day works, KI, Chat Bot, KI Agent, later the exercises. German, short, age-appropriate. Scratch colours and Scratch clipart (library art allowed with credit). Slides will be added over the coming days, so the system must make a new slide cheap.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The day is a sticker album to fill. Every slide is a full-bleed album page in one Scratch block colour; every idea is one die-cut Scratch sticker in its numbered slot. Refuses the white-slide-with-purple-bar-and-bullets school deck.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed pages in Scratch category colours (looks purple, motion blue, events yellow, operators green, my-blocks pink, sound magenta), one colour per page. Stickers: even white die-cut edge that follows the outline (SVG dilate), soft lifted shadow, slight rotation; each topic sticker sits in its own dashed slot. Slots: dashed rounded outlines in a lighter tint, numbered while empty, name plate under them. A chunky rounded display face (Lilita One) for headings, Lexend for text. Two content sizes (display, text) plus one small print size for captions and album chrome; a poem may sit between them. Rank otherwise from weight and colour (the cover's "Digitalferien 2026!" in light yellow). Page number bottom-right in a round badge, as printed albums do.

STORY: The kid recognises the Scratch world, feels welcomed, understands that the day is a collection they fill at their own pace (done = sticker, still to come = empty slot, nobody must fill every slot), then meets KI, Chat Bot and KI Agent as stickers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Purple album cover. Left two thirds: "Willkommen bei den Digitalferien 2026!" in the display face, very large, white with "Digitalferien 2026!" in light yellow. Right: Scratch cat sticker, tilted. Bottom: a row of dashed empty slots numbered with the pages to come. No buttons; Slidev arrows advance.
